What Koliya is

Koliya helps you plan a train journey between Ukraine and European countries. It shows direct trains and connections with a transfer, journey times and transfer stations, and explains where and how to buy tickets for each part of the trip.

Many routes consist of several trains run by different carriers, so the information normally has to be collected from several websites. Here it is gathered in one place in Russian, Ukrainian, English and Polish.

Where the data comes from

Timetables and route options are compiled from carriers’ information: Ukrzaliznytsia, PKP Intercity, RegioJet, České dráhy, ZSSK, MÁV, ÖBB, CFR Călători and Deutsche Bahn. The sources are listed in the footer, and the date the information was last updated is shown on the page.

Timetables change, so always check current information on the carrier’s website before you travel.

What Koliya does not do

We do not sell tickets or take payments. The “How to buy tickets” buttons lead to the carriers’ official websites and apps. Koliya is an independent information project and is not affiliated with JSC Ukrzaliznytsia or any other carrier.

Found a mistake?

If you notice an inaccuracy in a timetable or would like to suggest a new route, please write to us. It makes the site more useful for everyone.
