How to buy tickets

With a transfer in Przemyśl Główny

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Kharkiv → Przemyśl and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 09:00 Kyiv time. Choose the train and pay for the ticket.
  2. Buy the Przemyśl → Kraków ticket separately from the Polish carrier PKP Intercity, on intercity.pl or in the PKP Intercity app. You can also buy it on koleo.pl or in the KOLEO app.
  3. After buying, save both e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.
