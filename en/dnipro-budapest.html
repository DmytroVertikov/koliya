How to buy tickets

With a transfer in Chop

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Dnipro → Chop and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 08:00 Kyiv time. Choose train 003П and pay for the ticket.
  2. The train Chop → Budapest departs the next day, so ticket sales for it in the Ukrzaliznytsia app open the next day. You can also buy a ticket in advance from the Hungarian carrier MÁV, on jegy.mav.hu or in the MÁVPlusz app. MÁV tickets are usually cheaper.
  3. After buying, save both e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.
