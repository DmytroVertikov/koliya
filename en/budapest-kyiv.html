How to buy tickets

Direct train

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Budapest → Kyiv and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 08:00 Kyiv time.
  2. Pick a seat and pay by bank card. The e-ticket will appear in the app. Show it with your passport when boarding.
Buy the Budapest → Kyiv ticket in the UZ app

With a transfer in Chop

  1. Purchase a ticket for the Budapest → Chop train in the Ukrzaliznytsia app. Tickets go on sale 20 days before departure, at 08:00 Kyiv time. Choose the train you need and pay for your ticket. You can also purchase a ticket in advance from the Hungarian carrier MÁV — on the official website jegy.mav.hu or in the MÁVPlusz app. MÁV tickets are generally cheaper.
  2. Trains from Chop to Kyiv depart the following day, so tickets for these trains go on sale in the Ukrzaliznytsia app one day later.
  3. After buying, save both e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.
