In short

Koliya is a reference site, not a service with accounts. There is no registration and we do not set out to collect personal data. The data that reaches us is the messages sent through the feedback form and page ratings. The site shows no advertising, and visit statistics are switched on only with your consent.

What data we receive

  • Feedback form. If you send a message, we store its text, the chosen topic, the page address, the language and your contact (email or Telegram) if you provide one. We use it only to look into your message and reply to you. Please do not include unnecessary personal data.
  • “Was this helpful?” rating. We store the rating (yes or no), the page address and the language. It is not linked to a person.
  • Browser storage. So that we do not ask for a rating twice, your browser keeps a flag that you have already rated a page (localStorage). The site does not set its own cookies. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) appear only if you agree in the banner.

Third-party services

The site relies on third-party services. When pages load, they receive technical data about your request (for example your IP address and browser details) under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the site;
  • Supabase: the timetable database and the feedback form;
  • Google Fonts: fonts;
  • flagcdn.com: country flag images.
  • Google Analytics: visit statistics, only after your consent.

Links to carriers’ websites lead to external resources that have their own policies.

Advertising and analytics

The site shows no advertising. If you allow it in the banner, we use Google Analytics for visit statistics: which pages are opened, from which country and on which device. For this, Google sets cookies and receives data about your request, including your IP address, and may transfer it outside the EU. Without your consent no analytics is loaded. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the page.

Retention and your rights

Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the request. You can ask us to show, correct or delete your message and contact: write through the feedback form and say which message you mean. If you are in the EU or the UK, you also have the rights provided by the GDPR, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
