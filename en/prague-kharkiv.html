How to buy tickets

With a transfer in Chop

  1. Purchase a ticket for the Prague → Chop route on a RegioJet train operated by the Czech carrier RegioJet — on the official website regiojet.cz or in the RegioJet app.
  2. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Uzhhorod → Kharkiv and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 08:00 Kyiv time. Choose train 017Л and pay for the ticket.
  3. After buying, save both e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.
