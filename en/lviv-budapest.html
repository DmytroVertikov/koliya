How to buy tickets

Direct train

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Lviv → Budapest Keleti and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 08:00 Kyiv time.
  2. Pick a seat and pay by bank card. The e-ticket will appear in the app. Show it with your passport when boarding.
Buy the Lviv → Budapest Keleti ticket in the UZ app

With a transfer in Chop or Mukachevo

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the train you need on the Ukrainian part of the route (Lviv → Chop or Lviv → Mukachevo) and set the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure, at 08:00 Kyiv time. Pay for the ticket.
  2. Trains on the Hungarian section (Chop → Budapest and Mukachevo → Budapest) depart the next day, so ticket sales for them in the Ukrzaliznytsia app open the following day. You can also buy the ticket in advance from the Hungarian carrier MÁV, on jegy.mav.hu or in the MÁVPlusz app. MÁV tickets are usually cheaper.
  3. After buying, save both e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.

Train schedules may change. Check the latest information in the UZ app and on the MÁV website.
