How to buy tickets

With a transfer in Rava-Ruska

  1. Open the Ukrzaliznytsia app, choose the route Lviv → Warszawa Centralna and the travel date. Sales open 20 days before departure at 09:00 Kyiv time.
  2. Tap “З пересадками” (With transfers), then in the option offered pick seats for each train in turn and pay for both tickets in the app.
  3. After buying, save the e-tickets on your phone. Before you travel, check the dates, departure times and arrival stations.
